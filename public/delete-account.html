Delete your Wickglow account

Wickglow works without an account. If you made one to keep your progress on several devices, you can ask for it to be deleted at any time.

How to ask

  1. In the app, open Stats, find Account and tap Delete account. This opens an email with your account details filled in.
  2. Or send an email yourself to encarnacionelher@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Wickglow account", from the address you signed in with, and say which email the account uses.

What happens next

  • Your account is deleted 7 days after your request.
  • Deleted: your sign-in (email address and account id) and everything saved to the account: focus sessions, daily totals, and tasks.
  • Not deleted: progress kept on your own devices. Remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data in your browser.
  • Nothing is kept after deletion. Wickglow has no backups of account data.
