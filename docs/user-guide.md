# Wickglow user guide

Wickglow is a calm focus timer. You light a candle, study while it burns down, and take a break when it goes out. Lofi music and ambient sounds play while you work, and every focus minute earns XP toward your next level.

It runs on Android and in a web browser. You don't need an account.

## Your first session

1. Open Wickglow.
2. Tap the candle, or **Light the candle**. A 25-minute focus session starts.
3. Study. After a few seconds without a touch, the screen fades so only the candle and the time stay visible. Tap anywhere to bring everything back.
4. When the session ends, the flame dims, a soft chime plays and you get a notification. You earn XP and see a short message.
5. Tap **Start break** to begin your break, or **Later** to start it when you're ready.

That's the whole loop. The rest of this guide covers the options.

## The timer

### Modes

There are three modes: **Focus**, **Short break** and **Long break**. After each focus session you get a short break. After every fourth focus session you get a long break. The four small candles under the timer show how far you are toward the long break.

### Session lengths

Open **Timer** settings to pick a preset. Each preset button shows its focus and short break lengths:

| Preset button | Focus | Short break | Long break |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 25 / 5 (default) | 25 min | 5 min | 15 min |
| 50 / 10 | 50 min | 10 min | 20 min |
| 90 / 20 | 90 min | 20 min | 30 min |

You can also type your own lengths. Focus can be 1 to 180 minutes, a short break 1 to 60, and a long break 1 to 90.

### Controls

- **Pause and resume**: tap the main button.
- **Reset**: starts the current session over.
- **Skip**: moves on to the next mode.

A reset or skipped focus session earns nothing.

### Other timer settings

- **Start the next session automatically**: when a session ends, the next one starts on its own.
- **Hide the interface while I focus**: the fade during focus. Turn it off if you'd rather see everything all the time.
- **Music at session end**: what your music does when a focus session ends.
  - **Keep playing**
  - **Lower the volume** (default): drops the music to half until the next session starts.
  - **Fade out and stop**

  The chime plays with all three options, so you still hear the end if you aren't looking at the screen.

### Locked screens and backgrounding

The timer keeps correct time when you lock your phone or switch apps. When you come back it shows the right remaining time, or the finished session if it ended while you were away. On Android the notification arrives at the end of the session even with the screen off.

Wickglow asks for notification permission the first time you start a session. If you deny it, the timer still works, but you won't get a notification when a session ends.

## Tasks

Use tasks to keep track of what you're studying.

- **Add a task**: type a title, add a due date if you want one, and tap **Add**.
- **Focus on a task**: tap its title or its target button. The task then shows under the timer. Tap it again to clear it. You can also tap the line under the timer to pick a task.
- **Finish a task**: tick its checkbox. Finished tasks move under the open ones.
- **Edit or delete**: use the buttons on the task row.
- **Subtasks**: tap **+ Sub** to break a task into steps. Ticking the task ticks all its subtasks. Ticking the last open subtask finishes the task.
- **Due dates**: a task whose date has passed shows its date in flame orange. Due dates don't affect XP, streaks or quests.

Each task counts the focus sessions you finished while it was selected.

## Music and sounds

### Music

Wickglow comes with 14 lofi tracks. The player has previous, play or pause, next, a seek bar and a volume slider. Turn on **repeat** to loop the current track. With repeat off, the next track plays when one finishes.

Music keeps playing with the screen off.

### Your own music (Android)

Tap **Add files** next to the playlist to add audio files from your device. They join the playlist after the bundled tracks. They stay on your device, are never uploaded, and work offline. If you delete a file outside Wickglow, it is skipped. Adding your own files isn't available in the web version.

### Ambient sounds

The ambient mixer has nine sounds, each with its own volume slider. You can mix as many as you like:

Rain, Cafe, Fireplace, Wind, Clock, Brown noise, Forest, Keyboard, Waves.

Turn rain up and you'll see it falling on screen too.

### Streaming links (web, desktop size only)

In a wide browser window, you can paste a public Spotify, YouTube, YouTube Music or Apple Music link into the Music card. Wickglow saves the link and plays it in that service's own player.

- Only one thing plays at a time. Starting a stream pauses Wickglow's music, and playing a Wickglow track ends the stream.
- **End stream** stops the stream.
- If you aren't signed in to Spotify or Apple Music in your browser, they play a short preview.
- If a link can't play inside Wickglow, tap **Open link** to open it in the service's own site or app.
- YouTube Music links open in YouTube Music and don't play inside Wickglow.

Focus sessions count normally while a stream plays. Streaming isn't available in the Android app or in narrow browser windows.

## XP, levels, streaks and quests

### XP

Every completed focus minute earns **2 XP**. A 25-minute session earns 50 XP. Breaks earn nothing, and there's no daily limit.

### Levels

Level 1 takes 100 XP. Each level after that takes 50 XP more than the one before (150, then 200, and so on). As you level up, your title changes:

Novice → Apprentice → Lamplighter → Scholar → Sage → Archmage

### Streaks

Your streak goes up by one on the first day you finish a focus session of **at least 10 minutes**. Finish one every day to keep it going. If you miss a day, it resets.

Sessions shorter than 10 minutes still earn XP, but they don't start or keep a streak.

### Daily quests

Three quests reset at midnight. Each one pays out once a day:

| Quest | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Finish 4 focus sessions of at least 10 minutes | +40 XP |
| Study for 60 minutes | +50 XP |
| Complete 1 task | +30 XP |

Minutes from short sessions count toward the 60, but short sessions don't count toward the 4. A dot on the Stats tab tells you a quest is still open.

If you untick a task after it completed a quest, the quest and its XP are taken back.

## Stats and the calendar

**Stats** shows your level, total XP, today's minutes and sessions, a chart of the last 7 days, and your daily quests.

**Calendar** shows a month at a time. Days are shaded by how long you studied:

- no shade: nothing
- light: 1 to 24 minutes
- medium: 25 to 59 minutes
- deep: 60 minutes or more

Tap a day to see its minutes and sessions. Use the arrows to look at earlier months. Your streak and the month's total are shown too.

## Moods and scenes

A **mood** changes the colors of the app and the candle. There are seven: Amber (default), Moonlit, Rose, Violet, Ash, Forest and Tide.

On the desktop layout you can also pick a **scene**, an animated background behind the timer. On Android, a scene other than the default is downloaded the first time you pick it and then plays offline. Until the download finishes, the default scene plays. If a download fails, tap the tile to try again.

## Accounts and syncing

An account is optional. Without one, everything stays on your device.

With an account, your XP, level, streak, tasks and focus history stay the same on every device you sign in on, Android and web.

### Signing in

Open **Stats** and find the **Account** row. Tap **Sign in**, then either:

- enter your email and a password (at least 8 characters) and tap **Sign in** or **Create account**, or
- tap **Continue with Google**.

New email accounts get a confirmation email. The account works straight away, but the Account row shows **Email not confirmed** until you open the link. Tap **Resend** if you can't find the email. Use **Forgot password** to reset your password.

### Keeping your progress

If this device and your account both already have progress the first time you sign in, Wickglow asks **Keep which progress?** and shows both. Pick one. The other is replaced everywhere. If you aren't sure, tap **Decide later**. Sync stays off until you choose from the Account row.

### Sync status

The Account row shows where your progress stands:

- **Progress saved to your account** (green): everything is synced.
- **Saving to your account** or **Connecting…**: sync in progress.
- Amber: you're offline or still need to choose which progress to keep. Your changes stay on the device and sync when you're back online.
- Red: something went wrong. Tap **Try again**.

### What syncs and what doesn't

| Syncs | Stays on each device |
| --- | --- |
| XP, level, streak | Settings and timer lengths |
| Tasks and their done state | Sound levels and moods |
| Focus history and daily totals | Scenes, streaming links |
| | Subtasks, your own music files |

### Signing out

Signing out removes your progress (XP, streak, tasks and history) from that device and keeps your settings. Your progress is still safe in your account. If some changes haven't reached your account yet, Wickglow warns you first. You can then stay signed in until they sync, or sign out anyway and lose them.

### Deleting your account

Tap **Delete account** in the Account row. It opens an email request. You can also ask through the page at <https://wickglow.vercel.app/delete-account>.

## Phone, tablet and desktop

Wickglow changes its layout to fit the screen:

- **Phone**: the candle and timer fill the screen. The tab bar at the bottom (Tasks, Sounds, Stats, Mood, Timer) opens each section as a sheet over the timer.
- **Tablet**, or a phone turned sideways: the timer is on one side, and tasks, sounds, quests, stats and settings sit in a column on the other.
- **Desktop browser**: a full-screen scene with a large timer and a row of buttons along the bottom. Each button opens a card for Music, Progress, Quests, Tasks, sounds and scenes, or timer settings. A **Full screen** button appears when your browser supports it.

## Privacy

- No ads, analytics or tracking.
- Wickglow never records audio and doesn't ask for microphone access.
- Without an account, nothing leaves your device.
- With an account, your email (and your name, if you signed in with Google), focus sessions and tasks are stored with Firebase to sync your progress.

The full policy is at <https://wickglow.vercel.app/privacy>.

## FAQ

**Do I need an account?**
No. Everything works without one. An account only adds syncing between devices.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. The timer, tasks, bundled music, ambient sounds and progress all work offline. Syncing waits until you're back online. Streaming links and scene downloads need a connection.

**Why didn't my session count toward my streak?**
Only sessions of at least 10 minutes count toward the streak and the 4-sessions quest. Shorter sessions still earn XP and count toward the 60-minute quest.

**Why didn't I get XP?**
You get XP only for focus sessions that run to the end. Reset or skipped sessions earn nothing, and so do breaks.

**I didn't get a notification when my session ended.**
Check that notifications are allowed for Wickglow in your phone's settings. Battery saver settings on some phones can also delay them.

**My music stopped when the session ended.**
Check **Music at session end** in Timer settings. **Fade out and stop** ends the music. Choose **Keep playing** or **Lower the volume** instead.

**Spotify only plays 30 seconds.**
Sign in to Spotify in the same browser. Signed out, Spotify and Apple Music only play previews.

**I signed out and my progress is gone.**
It's still in your account. Sign in again on that device and it comes back.

**How do I start over?**
Without an account, uninstall the app or clear the site's data in your browser. This deletes everything on that device.
