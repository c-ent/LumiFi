Wickglow privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Wickglow is a focus timer with music, ambient sounds, tasks and progress. It works without an account. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never records audio.

Without an account

Everything you do stays on your device: focus sessions, XP, level, streak, tasks, settings, the music and ambient levels you pick, and any streaming links you paste. Music files you add from your own device are played from your device and are never uploaded.

To remove it, uninstall the app, or clear the site's data in your browser.

With an account

An account is optional. It keeps your progress the same on several devices. Accounts use Firebase, a Google service (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore).

  • Sign-in: your email address, an account id, and your name if you add one or sign in with Google. With email sign-in, your password is handled by Firebase and Wickglow never sees it.
  • Saved progress: your focus sessions (when they happened and how long), daily totals, and your tasks (their titles and whether they are done). This is how XP, level, streak and quests are worked out on each device.
  • Not saved to the account: settings, sound levels, scenes, streaming links, subtasks and music files.

Firebase may also log technical details such as your IP address when the app connects to it, as described in Firebase's privacy and security information.

Your account data is used only to sync your progress. It is not sold, shared for advertising, or used to profile you.

Background scenes

Some background videos are downloaded from the Wickglow website, hosted on Vercel, when you pick them. Like any website, Vercel sees your device's IP address when it sends the file. Nothing else is sent.

Streaming players

On the website, you can paste a public Spotify, YouTube or Apple Music link and play it inside Wickglow. The player is that service's own embed, so the service loads in your browser and may set its own cookies, including third-party cookies, under its own privacy policy. YouTube players use the reduced-cookie address youtube-nocookie.com. On Android, a pasted link opens in that service's app instead.

To manage these cookies, use your browser's settings: you can block third-party cookies or clear a site's cookies there. If you never play a streaming link, these services are not loaded. Their policies: Spotify, Google (YouTube), Apple.

Notifications

Wickglow can show a notification when a focus session or break ends. It is scheduled on your device. No push service is used.

Deleting your data

You can ask for your account and everything saved to it to be deleted at any time. See Delete your Wickglow account. Data on your own devices is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data.

Children

Wickglow is meant for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and an account should not be made for a child under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, write to the address below and it will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. A change that affects how your data is used will also be noted in the app.
